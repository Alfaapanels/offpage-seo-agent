## 7. Notes & Caveats

- All directory links should be verified monthly to ensure they remain live.
- Prioritise niche-relevant links (cold storage, prefab panels, construction) over generic directories.
- Avoid link farms or low-quality mass submission services; a few strong links in the right niche outperform 100 generic ones.
- Track new links in a spreadsheet: date acquired, source URL, anchor text, do-follow/no-follow status.
- Domain-level backlink profile (DA, referring domains count) should be checked monthly via Ahrefs or SEMrush.
