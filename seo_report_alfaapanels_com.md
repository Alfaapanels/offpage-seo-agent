## Company Profile Summary

Alfaa Panels (Alfaa PEB Ltd) is one of India's leading manufacturers of insulated building panels. Their product range includes:
- Insulated PUF/PIR sandwich panels (30mm–150mm thickness)
- Cold room wall, ceiling, and door panels
- Clean room partition panels
- Fireproof rockwool panels
- Pre-engineered building (PEB) solutions

**Key markets:** Food processing, cold chain, clean rooms, prefabricated structures, pharma, industrial construction.
