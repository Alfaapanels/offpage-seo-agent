>
> PUF (Polyurethane Foam) sandwich panels are composite building components made of two pre-painted galvanised steel skins bonded to a rigid polyurethane foam core. Here are the main benefits:
>
> **Thermal Insulation:** PUF has a thermal conductivity of ~0.022 W/mK — much better than EPS or glass wool. Facilities using PUF panels report 30–40% lower HVAC energy costs.
>
> **Speed of construction:** Panels arrive pre-fabricated and lock together with cam-lock joints — a typical warehouse shell can be erected in days, not weeks.
>
> **Long life:** High-quality PUF panels last 25+ years. PIR (Polyisocyanurate) variants offer up to 50 years.
>
> **Applications in India:** Cold storage, pharmaceutical clean rooms, food processing, warehouses, industrial sheds, prefab buildings.
>
> **Cost:** In India (2026), 80mm cold storage PUF panels run ₹180–220/sq ft; 100mm frozen storage grade runs ₹215–265/sq ft.
>
